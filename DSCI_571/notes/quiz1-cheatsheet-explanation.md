## Content decisions

The page follows the model-development lifecycle rather than lecture order: task/data → estimator/metrics → baseline/tree → CV → similarity models → preprocessing/pipelines. It prioritizes distinctions needed for reasoning questions and code tracing: classification vs regression, parameter vs hyperparameter, validation vs test, R² vs accuracy, k/C/gamma effects, transformer fit boundaries, feature-type encodings, and fold-local preprocessing.

Dates, grades, platform actions, sources, status, and integrity language are excluded from the exam-facing page. Generic templates are retained; no Lab 2 feature list, answer, CV result, or final model is supplied.
