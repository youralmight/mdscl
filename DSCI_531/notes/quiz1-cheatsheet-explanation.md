# DSCI 531 Quiz 1 Cheat Sheet Explanation

> **Not intended for upload or exam use.** This companion keeps scope evidence, source links, and compression decisions outside the exam-facing [cheat sheet](quiz1-cheatsheet.md).

## Scope status

The content is compressed from [Week 1 knowledge](week1-knowledge.md) and [Week 2 knowledge](week2-knowledge.md): current Lecture 1–4 required readings, Ch1–10, Ch12, and Ch15; Ch11 remains optional. It covers grammar/channels, groups/facets/connections, summaries, counts/proportions, histogram/KDE, joint distributions, and EDA in both Altair and ggplot2.

The authenticated PrairieLearn course currently exposes **Practice Quiz 1**, but starting it requires certifying the student's identity and honor pledge, so no question was opened. No Quiz 1 cheatsheet upload assessment was visible at review time. Therefore the exact Quiz 1 topic list and permission to use a cheatsheet remain **unconfirmed**; this is a preparation draft until the instructor/assessment explicitly permits it.

If permitted, the general MDS rule is one one-sided US Letter page, minimum 8 pt, submitted as a PNG no larger than 5 MB. The rendered PNG follows those physical constraints, but course-specific instructions supersede them.

## Content decisions

The exam-facing file keeps only reusable decision rules, formulas, traps, and compact code patterns. It deliberately omits dates, weights, platform instructions, sources, document status, integrity rules, and editorial history. Both languages are retained because the current course and Labs use Altair and ggplot2.

Priority was given to distinctions that are easy to confuse under time pressure: mapping vs fixed styling; field semantics; layer vs facet; line grouping/order; spread vs uncertainty; count denominator; histogram bins vs KDE bandwidth; density vs count; joint vs conditional quantities; and EDA conclusions versus causal claims.
