## Content-selection and compression decisions

The Cheat Sheet retains facts useful for solving likely probability questions: notation; PMF/PDF distinctions; event laws; odds; expectation, variance, entropy, and transformations; distribution supports/PMFs/moments/R signatures; parameter recovery; joint/marginal/conditional calculations; independence and conditional-independence tests; covariance, Pearson correlation, and Kendall's \(\tau\); and pitfalls such as collision aggregation under \(g(X)\), the geometric convention, and zero correlation not proving independence.

Explanatory paragraphs were compressed into formulas, decision rules, and short traps. Process descriptions were kept only where they directly prescribe a calculation (for example, normalize after conditioning or check every joint-PMF cell for independence). Scope/status evidence, platform details, integrity policy, sources, document-reading advice, and pre-exam checklist framing were excluded so the exam-facing artifact begins with usable course content and remains space-efficient.
