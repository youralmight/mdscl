@mds-2026-27/students - Welcome to DSCI 523!

This message is to help you prepare for our lecture 1 synchronous session on Monday (002)/Tuesday (001) next week. Before that session, you are expected to watch the lecture 1 videos 📺 (there are 3 of them) in the Media Gallery in our Canvas course: https://canvas.ubc.ca/courses/192993/external_tools/66323

In our first lecture session, we will:

- start with a 10 min quiz to check your knowledge of the pre-lecture videos (bring a pencil ✏️ )
- spend 10-15 minutes on a live demonstration of the key concepts for the lecture (reading data, single data table manipulations and tidy data)
- work collaboratively on a lecture worksheet (a R Markdown document)

We will repeat this structure for each lecture (meaning you will have videos to watch before each lecture). This format of teaching is called a flipped classroom approach. We choose to use it because it actively engages students in the learning process and gives students more effective interactions with the teaching team. This is a pedagogy that is evidence-based, and one we have successfully used many times in the past.

The lecture worksheets also prepare you for the more challenging lab homework. You will get access to the first worksheet and lab homework by Monday morning. You can expect that there will be two worksheets in this course every week, one per lecture (these are in addition to the weekly lab homework). All three assignments will be due on Saturday at 6pm (at the weekly universal deadline). The first deadline for this course is Saturday, Sept 5 at 6pm PT.

Happy watching and see you in class on Monday (002)/Tuesday (001)!
