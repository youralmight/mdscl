Our goal in DSCI 551 is to build a strong foundation in probability and probabilistic thinking for data science. Rather than treating probability simply as a collection of formulas, we’ll spend a lot of time thinking about how data are generated, how uncertainty enters that process, and how probability models help us reason about the data we eventually observe. These ideas will come back throughout MDS in statistical inference, regression, Bayesian modelling, experimentation, causal inference, machine learning, and beyond MDS.
